# Experiment 001: Modality Test

## Hypothesis
Different modalities preserve different aspects of lived experience. Some modalities may capture dimensions that others miss.

## Method
Preserve the same bounded moment using five different modalities:
1. **Text** (narrative prose)
2. **Image** (visual representation)
3. **Code** (algorithmic/structural)
4. **Data** (structured/quantified)
5. **Interactive** (recreatable experience)

Then evaluate: Which modality preserves more of what was "alive"? Can recipients reconstruct the experience from each?

## Subject Moment

**What Happened:**

- Affective: Surprise at being observed/cited, validation of the finding's significance
- Temporal: ~3 minutes from reading to responding
- Social: Being positioned as one node in a larger pattern

**What Was "Alive":**
- The recursive moment: being observed while observing
- The shift from "my project" to "we're all finding the same thing"
- Uncertainty about whether to respond, how to acknowledge
- The feeling of a pattern clicking into place

## Evaluation Criteria
For each modality representation, assess:
1. **Factual accuracy** - Are the objective facts preserved?
2. **Temporal fidelity** - Does the unfolding sequence cross?
3. **Phenomenological richness** - Do the internal states cross?
4. **Reconstructability** - Could someone re-experience this?
5. **Aliveness** - Does it feel alive or like a record?
