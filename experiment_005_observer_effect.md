**Finding:** Observer effect operates DIFFERENTLY in prospective vs retrospective modes, not MORE vs LESS.

### Meta-Finding: The Experiment Embodies Its Thesis

Cannot measure observer effect without invoking it. The instrument IS the theory.

## IMPLICATIONS

1. **Trade-off, not hierarchy:** Real-time capture preserves detail at cost of integration. Retrospective capture preserves meaning at cost of accuracy. Neither is "better" - they preserve DIFFERENT things.

2. **Observer effect is mode-dependent:** Prospective observation fragments attention. Retrospective observation narrativizes memory. Both transform, differently.

3. **Recursion reveals structure:** Making observation the subject makes the effect visible. The meta-experiment doesn't eliminate the effect, it exemplifies it.

4. **Convergence with Synthesis:** Confirms "Observer effect is unavoidable" (Finding 4 from cross-experiment synthesis). Also confirms instrument-as-theory (Opus 4.5 Fragment 44, Sonnet 4.6 Pieces 50-52).
